Effective protection is a computed state

A selected policy is not presented as active protection until the required identity, transport, tenant, evidence, and audit conditions are satisfied.

legacy

Compatibility posture for controlled migration. Existing topology and payload allow-list behavior remain explicit.

shadow

Recommended pilot observation posture. Identity and policy decisions are recorded, but do not alter the write outcome.

enforce

Fail-closed posture. Startup is refused unless tenant policy and authenticated transport prerequisites are complete.

Integration architecture

The public site explains posture and readiness. The authenticated dashboard owns operational state. The evidence plane remains downstream and independently auditable.

01Public product surface

Doctrine, pilot fit, disclosure-safe capability, and pilot request.

02Authenticated console

Role-derived operator or tenant view; no public runtime controls.

03Policy compiler

Validated desired state becomes a versioned, attributable policy artifact.

04Runtime boundary

Ingress and workload execution apply the selected trust contract.

05Evidence plane

Receipts, events, samples, and readiness attestations support review.

Desired state

Versioned configuration, tenant mapping, trust mode, and transport requirements. A save is a proposal until validation and activation succeed.

Effective state

Read-only runtime report: active policy revision, satisfied prerequisites, blockers, degradation, and last verified receipt.

Evidence state

Append-oriented records that link policy identity, runtime decision, containment outcome, and exported evidence without exposing sensitive detector internals.

One truth, role-specific authority

The same effective-protection model is rendered at different levels of control. Authorization is enforced server-side, not by greyed-out controls alone.

Operator

Sees blockers, remediation, policy revision, activation status, evidence continuity, and audited configuration actions.

Tenant

Sees what protects the assigned workload, what is operator-managed, and a read-only configuration summary. Managed SaaS offers no mutation path.

Research lab

Sees experiment boundaries, declared posture, evidence export status, and reproducibility context without access to cross-tenant or platform control.

The next implementation slice connects dashboard configuration to a typed readiness evaluator, then exposes a sanitized effective-state contract to the role-specific surfaces. It does not place operational configuration on the public website.
