Konsonans AI Governance

A policy-as-code control plane for agentic systems that need traceable decisions, bounded autonomy, and operator hold points under the EU AI Act.

policy gateaudit chainhuman approval

Core offer

  • Typed risk tiers for tool-calls, plans, and budget actions.
  • Hash-chained execution records with signable receipt output.
  • Hold, approve, and timeout flows for higher-risk actions.

Deployment fit

  • Operator consoles that need accountable AI assistance.
  • Financial, compliance, and documentation workflows.
  • Teams that need policy updates without rewriting the agent runtime.

Near-term shape

  • GraphQL and policy APIs for control and reporting.
  • Monetary planning trails aligned with approval checkpoints.
  • EU AI Act article mapping kept close to execution evidence.

The product direction is operational governance rather than abstract policy dashboards: each action is evaluated, logged, and surfaced in a way an operator can actually review.
