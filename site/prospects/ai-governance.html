What is done

The pilot path is implemented as an executable governance and evidence workflow, not only a specification.

  • Typed policy gates, identity-aware access, approval queues, audit views, and operator controls are implemented across the control-plane surfaces.
  • A self-contained buyer demonstration drives allow, hold, approval, and denial decisions and exports a signed real-workload evidence pack for independent verification.
  • Release, demo, and deployment preflight profiles fail closed on missing requirements; evidence manifests can be signed with Ed25519 or a configured PKCS#11/HSM backend.

What is left

The remaining gates depend on the target buyer environment and independent review, not another invented compliance percentage.

  • Run and preserve the validation packet inside the final buyer or operator boundary with buyer-owned identity, retention, key custody, and production integrations.
  • Obtain buyer legal, security, HA/SLA, and—where applicable—notified-body review of the deployment-specific evidence and residual risks.
  • Turn the verified pilot deployment into an agreed service scope, support model, and buyer-specific conformity process.

Commercial frame

The value is operational accountability: each action can be reviewed, signed, delayed, or rejected without rewriting the agent runtime.

  • Target use: financial, compliance, and documentation workflows where bounded autonomy matters.
  • Pricing signal: €25–250/mo per agent, matching the current product line estimate.
  • Near-term sale shape: managed governance layer for teams already using AI agents in high-consequence operations.

The product is strongest when it stays close to execution: policy, approval, audit trail, and operator review in one path instead of scattered dashboards.
