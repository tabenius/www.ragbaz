Articulate

A hardened commerce and CMS stack that keeps WordPress private, places the public surface behind controlled runtimes, and automates the surrounding delivery path.

gatekeeperstorefronttenant provisioner

Core offer

  • Private authoring origin with gateway control and immutable runtime boundaries.
  • Next.js storefront delivery with controlled public routes.
  • DNS, billing, and tenant workflow tied into the same operational lane.

Why it exists

  • WordPress remains the content plane, not the public attack surface.
  • Image, filesystem, and runtime hardening happen outside the editor UI.
  • Operators need a coherent delivery stack, not disconnected hosting pieces.

Current focus

  • Provisioning reliability across routes, DNS, and mail.
  • Storefront/runtime alignment on Cloudflare Worker delivery.
  • Safer content publishing and cache invalidation workflows.

Articulate is less “headless WordPress” in the generic sense and more a controlled operating environment for WordPress-backed products.
