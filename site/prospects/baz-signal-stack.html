BAZ Signal Stack

A three-part systematic trading toolkit: glither.hft for strategy compilation, Palantir for orchestration and enrichment, and Luna for notebook-grade browser inspection.

compilerorchestrationnotebook widgets

Core offer

  • Compile glither.hft strategies to WASM with signed monetary audit receipts.
  • Palantir manages enrichment, streaming, and signal transport.
  • Luna provides browser-native notebook and charting surfaces for inspection.

Why it fits

  • Teams that want executable strategy artifacts, not only notebooks.
  • Auditability around strategy execution and monetary planning.
  • Operator tools that can move between analysis and runtime review.

Current focus

  • Dialect maturation around baz.hft and glither.hft.
  • WebAssembly bridge work for browser/runtime parity.
  • Luna-style widgets that surface streaming signals cleanly.

The point is not just to render charts. It is to keep the strategy language, the execution receipts, and the operator notebook in one traceable lane.
