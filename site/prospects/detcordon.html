DetCordon

Containment-first observation for hostile web payloads: disposable sandboxes, isolated network posture, and structured capture of what the payload actually did.

sandboxrecorderioc extraction

Core offer

  • Disposable execution lanes separated from analyst workstation state.
  • Network, DOM, storage, and file-system capture tied to the same run.
  • Structured indicators and reports instead of raw logs only.

Why it matters

  • Unknown payloads should not need to touch your everyday machine.
  • Observation is useful only when the evidence survives export and review.
  • Repeatable containment matters more than one-off heroic detonation setups.

Current focus

  • Cleaner operator workflow between sandbox, sink, and reports.
  • Telemetry capture that stays usable under hostile content.
  • Evidence packaging for downstream governance and archival systems.

DetCordon is aimed at operational triage: get the evidence out, keep the blast radius small, and produce output that another analyst can use.
