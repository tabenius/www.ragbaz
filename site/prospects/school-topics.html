RAGBAZ School Topics

A field-school surface with two active modules: cellular trust and transport analysis, plus a guided Android compromise-assessment course for practitioners working on hardware they control.

cellularforensicsteacher manual

Cellular

SIM and eUICC trust, sender provenance limits, covert transport channels, incident-response framing, and field-ready terminology.

  • Trust and identity paths from SIM to network.
  • SMS, MMS, and operator-side provenance limits.
  • Reference material built for repeated operational use.
Open cellular

Forensics

Android compromise assessment with five labs, a teacher’s manual, and read-only acquisition tools oriented toward practical review rather than spectacle.

  • Rootkit, RAT, stalkerware, and backdoor hunting.
  • Hands-on labs with operator notes and sequencing.
  • Read-only acquisition posture where possible.
Open forensics

The school is meant to stay close to product and research work, so topic material can double as operational reference instead of floating away into generic courseware.
