Customer responsibilities

  • Approve all samples, fixtures, data-handling rules, and retention periods
  • Maintain analyst private keys and control decrypted evidence
  • Supply measured baseline data for the value model and success criteria

Acceptance evidence

  • Deployment passes agreed host and topology checks
  • An approved workload produces attributable events and evidence
  • Evidence export verifies offline without repository access
  • Requested and effective protection posture are reviewed together
  • Customer operators repeat the workflow from the written runbook
  • Residual risks and production gaps are documented rather than hidden

Explicitly excluded

  • Offensive operations, exploit development, or targeting third parties
  • A formal proof that sandbox escape is impossible
  • Managed 24/7 monitoring, uptime SLA, or incident-response retainer
  • Unlimited customization, multi-region HA, or production certification
  • Custody of the customer’s private decryption keys
  • Any guarantee of savings, detection rate, or regulatory outcome
